ParaKit: Investigation Toolkit Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-02

ParaKit: Investigation Toolkit is a personal investigation logging tool. It does not send your personal data, evidence, or location data to a server controlled by us or anyone else.

Summary

No data leaves your device. The app does not transmit personal data, evidence, audio, photos, video, or location information to any server controlled by us or by anyone else.

What the app accesses

Data Why Where it goes
Approximate / precise location To stamp each investigation with a location and fetch weather conditions. Stored on your device. Weather is fetched from open-meteo.com using latitude and longitude only.
Microphone To record audio for EVP review. Saved to the app's private storage on your device.
Camera To capture still photos and video as evidence. Saved to the app's private storage on your device.
Magnetometer To measure ambient magnetic field for EMF readings. Stored as numeric readings on your device.
Accelerometer To detect surface vibrations. Stored as numeric readings on your device.
Internet One outbound request per investigation start, to fetch weather for the captured GPS coordinates. No personal identifiers are sent - only latitude and longitude.

Where data is stored

All investigation data is stored on your device. Media files live in the app's private filesystem area, and are accessible only to ParaKit: Investigation Toolkit.

What we do not collect

  • No accounts, sign-ups, or logins.
  • No analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party SDKs.
  • No advertising identifiers.
  • No usage telemetry sent to us.
  • No crash reports sent to us.

Permissions

When you use a sensor or evidence-capture tool, Android will ask you to grant the relevant permission. You can revoke those permissions at any time in Settings.

Deleting your data

  • Deleting an investigation removes its metadata and associated media files from your device.
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything stored in the app sandbox.

Third-party services

The only outbound connection the app makes is to open-meteo.com to fetch weather. See open-meteo terms.
